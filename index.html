Welcome to ICSCC 2027

Advancing Smart Computing, Communications & Intelligent Systems

The International Conference on Smart Computing and Communications (ICSCC 2027) is a premier international forum bringing together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to exchange breakthrough ideas and advancements.

With our theme “Intelligent Technologies for Smart and Sustainable Futures”, the conference emphasizes the transformative role of intelligent computing in building resilient, sustainable, and interconnected solutions across Computer Science, Electronics, AI, and Power Systems.

Conference Scope

6 Key Technical Tracks

We invite original, unpublished experimental and theoretical research papers spanning intelligent technologies and smart systems.

Track 01

Smart Computing

AI, machine learning, quantum computing, high-performance computing (HPC), algorithms, cloud/edge computing, and modern computational technologies.

Track 02

Communication & Signal Processing

Track 03

Technology for Secure & Sustainable World

Track 04

Intelligent Systems & Analytics
