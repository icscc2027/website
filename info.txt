About ICSCC 2027
Welcome to the International Conference on Smart Computing and Communications (ICSCC 2027), a premier international forum bringing together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to exchange ideas and advancements in smart computing, communication technologies, and intelligent systems. Scheduled to be held in Kochi, Kerala, India, ICSCC 2027 aims to foster collaboration between academia and industry while showcasing cutting-edge research, practical applications, and emerging technological trends.
With the theme “Intelligent Technologies for Smart and Sustainable Futures”, ICSCC 2027 focuses on the transformative role of intelligent technologies in building sustainable, efficient, and connected systems across diverse domains including Computer Science, Communication, Electronics, Artificial Intelligence, and Energy Systems. The conference will feature keynote talks, technical paper presentations, workshops, and interactive sessions designed to inspire innovation and knowledge sharing among global participants.
Building on its strong legacy as a distinguished conference series, ICSCC continues to serve as a vibrant platform for discussing state-of-the-art developments and future directions in smart technologies. Researchers and practitioners are invited to contribute high-quality theoretical and experimental work and join us in shaping the future of intelligent and sustainable technologies.
