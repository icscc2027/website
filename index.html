About the Conference

The International Conference on Smart Computing and Communications (ICSCC) is a premier annual forum that brings together leading researchers, practitioners, and innovators from academia and industry across the globe. Now in its 13th edition, ICSCC 2027 continues the tradition of fostering cutting-edge research and collaborative dialogue in the fields of intelligent computing, next-generation communications, and transformative digital technologies.

Organized by the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering at Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Kochi, the conference is technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Kerala Section. All accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore Digital Library, subject to peer review and quality standards.

ICSCC 2027 will feature keynote addresses from world-renowned experts, technical paper presentations, special sessions, panel discussions, workshops, and demonstrations covering six thematic tracks aligned with the conference theme: Intelligent Technologies for a Sustainable and Connected World.

Scope & Topics

ICSCC 2027 invites original, unpublished research papers from researchers, scientists, engineers, and practitioners. Papers are solicited across the following six tracks:

Track 1
Smart Computing

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Neural Networks, Natural Language Processing, Cloud Computing, Fog/Edge Computing, Quantum Computing, High-Performance Computing, Parallel & Distributed Systems, Computer Vision, Augmented/Virtual Reality.

Track 2
Communication & Signal Processing

5G/6G Networks, Wireless & Mobile Communications, OFDM, MIMO Systems, Cognitive Radio, Antenna Design, Digital Signal Processing, Image & Video Processing, Optical Communications, Satellite Communications.

Track 3
Technology (Systems for a secure and sustainable world)

Cybersecurity, Network Security, Blockchain, Cryptography, Privacy-Preserving Computing, Sustainable Engineering, Green Computing, Environmental Monitoring Systems, Disaster Management Technologies.

Track 4
Intelligent Systems & Analytics

Data Analytics, Big Data, Business Intelligence, Robotics, Autonomous Systems, Smart Healthcare, Affective Computing, Decision Support Systems, Knowledge Engineering.

Track 5
Smart Embedded Systems

Internet of Things, FPGA Design, VLSI & SoC, Real-Time Embedded Systems, Wearable Computing, Sensor Networks, Microcontroller Applications, MEMS, Smart Agriculture, Smart Cities.

Track 6
Power Systems, Energy Systems & Power Electronics

Smart Grid, Renewable Energy, Energy Storage, Power Converters, Motor Drives, Electric Vehicles, Energy Harvesting, Distributed Generation, FACTS Devices.

Publication in IEEE Xplore

All accepted and registered papers presented at ICSCC 2027 will be submitted for publication in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library, one of the world's most comprehensive repositories of scientific and technical literature in electrical engineering, electronics, and computer science.

Published papers will be indexed in Scopus and Web of Science, ensuring maximum visibility and citation potential for your research. Each paper will receive a unique Digital Object Identifier (DOI).
