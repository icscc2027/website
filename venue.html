Conference Venue

Muthoot Institute of Technology and Science (MITS)

“Rooted in Tradition, Engineered for the Future”

MITS is situated in the industrial suburb of Kochi, close to the Smart City and InfoPark, approximately 15 kms from Vytilla Junction towards Muvattupuzha on the Cochin Madurai National Highway.

The promoters, with their commanding presence in the domains of Financial Services, Hospitality, Healthcare, Renewable Energy, and Information Technology, are committed to building MITS as a centre of excellence, focusing purely on merit.

Transportation

How to Reach MITS, Kochi

By Air (COK)

Cochin International Airport (~35 km)

Cochin Airport is well-connected with non-stop flights to the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Europe, and all major Indian metros. Prepaid taxis and Uber/Ola cabs are readily available at the airport terminal to reach MITS Puthencruz in ~45–60 minutes.

By Rail

ERS / ERN / Aluva Stations (~22–25 km)

Ernakulam Junction (South - ERS) and Ernakulam Town (North - ERN) are major railway hubs connected across India. Aluva Railway Station is also conveniently located along the highway route.

By Metro & Road

Cochin-Madurai NH (NH 85)

Take the Kochi Metro to Petta or SN Junction stations. From there, auto-rickshaws, city buses, and taxis connect along the Cochin-Madurai Highway towards Puthencruz (15 kms from Vytilla Mobility Hub).
