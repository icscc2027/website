Keynote Speakers & Experts

Keynote 01 Smart Computing

Prof. Vincenzo Piuri

Fellow of IEEE • University of Milan, Italy

Keynote Address:

“Artificial Intelligence & Edge Paradigms for Ambient Intelligence”

Presents transformative architectures integrating lightweight deep learning on resource-constrained sensors and ambient computing ecosystems.

Day 1 • 3 July 2027
Keynote 02 6G Communications

Prof. Mohammad S. Obaidat

Fellow of IEEE & Fellow of SCS • Univ. of Texas / Jordan

Keynote Address:

“Next-Generation 6G Wireless and Autonomous Connected Vehicles”

Explores terahertz wireless channels, integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), and ultra-reliable low latency protocols for vehicular autonomy.

Day 2 • 4 July 2027
Keynote 03 Cybersecurity & IoT

Dr. Joel J. P. C. Rodrigues

Fellow of IEEE • Senac Faculty of Ceará, Brazil

Keynote Address:

“Trustworthy IoT Architectures & Medical Sensor Security”

Addresses vulnerabilities in healthcare telematics, wearable bio-sensors, and robust cryptographic defenses for distributed edge devices.

Day 2 • 4 July 2027
Keynote 04 Energy & Smart Grids

Dr. Sudeep Tanwar

Senior Member IEEE • Nirma University, India

Keynote Address:

“Blockchain & AI for Resilient Decentralized Smart Energy Grids”

Highlights peer-to-peer renewable microgrid trading, smart contract automation, and load balancing algorithms in EV charging ecosystems.

Day 3 • 5 July 2027
