Author Guidelines
Track 01

Smart Computing

Computational Intelligence & Architectures

Focuses on theoretical algorithms and innovative computational technologies including AI, machine learning, quantum paradigms, HPC, algorithms, cloud and edge computing systems.

Topics of Interest:

Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning Machine Learning Algorithms Quantum Computing & Algorithms High Performance Computing (HPC) Cloud, Fog & Edge Computing Advanced Computational Technologies Theoretical Computer Science
Track 02

Communication & Signal Processing

Next-Gen Networking & Signal Theory

Addresses advancements in 5G/6G communications, networking architectures, optical & wireless transmission, protocol designs, digital signal processing, and network optimization.

Topics of Interest:

5G, 6G & Beyond Wireless Networks Communication Protocols & Architectures Wireless Sensor & Ad-hoc Networks Digital, Image & Video Signal Processing Software Defined Networking (SDN) MIMO, Beamforming & Antenna Systems Network Performance & Quality of Service (QoS)
Track 03

Technology (Systems for a Secure and Sustainable World)

Cybersecurity, Privacy & Green Computing

Explores the convergence of connected Internet of Things (IoT) systems, digital privacy, robust cybersecurity mechanisms, trustworthy computing frameworks, and environmentally sustainable digital technologies.

Topics of Interest:

Internet of Things (IoT) Architectures Cybersecurity & Threat Detection Privacy-Preserving Technologies & Cryptography Blockchain & Distributed Ledger Security Sustainable & Green Computing Systems Critical Infrastructure Protection
Track 04

Intelligent Systems & Analytics

Big Data & Applied Analytics

Encompasses modern data science, scalable big-data pipelines, predictive modeling, knowledge discovery, text/sentiment analytics, and intelligent domain applications in healthcare, smart cities, and governance.

Topics of Interest:

Big Data Analytics & Data Engineering Knowledge Discovery & Data Mining Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics Natural Language Processing (NLP) & GenAI Computer Vision & Multimodal Analytics Healthcare Informatics & Smart City Analytics
Track 05

Smart Embedded Systems

Hardware & Embedded Intelligence

Covers advanced hardware systems, VLSI circuits, FPGA accelerators, System-on-Chip (SoC) architectures, intelligent sensor interfaces, real-time operating systems, and embedded edge AI computing.

Topics of Interest:

Embedded Systems Architecture VLSI Design & Test Methodologies FPGA & Reconfigurable Computing System-on-Chip (SoC) & NoC Intelligent Sensors & Actuators Embedded AI / TinyML Accelerators Real-Time Embedded Operating Systems
Track 06

Power Systems, Energy Systems and Power Electronics

Energy Transition & Smart Grids

Addresses modern power engineering challenges, renewable energy integration (solar, wind), intelligent smart grids, energy storage management, Electric Vehicles (EVs) powertrain, and high-efficiency power electronic converters.

Topics of Interest:

Smart Grids & Microgrid Operations Renewable Energy Integration Battery Management & Energy Storage Electric Vehicles (EV) & Charging Infrastructure Power Electronic Converters & Inverters Power Quality & Modern Protection Systems AI Applications in Energy Systems
