International Forum

International Conference on Smart Computing and Communications (ICSCC 2027)

Theme: “Intelligent Technologies for Smart and Sustainable Futures”

Welcome to the International Conference on Smart Computing and Communications (ICSCC 2027), a premier international forum bringing together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to exchange ideas and advancements in smart computing, communication technologies, and intelligent systems.

Scheduled to be held in Kochi, Kerala, India, ICSCC 2027 aims to foster collaboration between academia and industry while showcasing cutting-edge research, practical applications, and emerging technological trends.

With the theme “Intelligent Technologies for Smart and Sustainable Futures”, ICSCC 2027 focuses on the transformative role of intelligent technologies in building sustainable, efficient, and connected systems across diverse domains including Computer Science, Communication, Electronics, Artificial Intelligence, and Energy Systems.

Building on its strong legacy as a distinguished conference series, ICSCC continues to serve as a vibrant platform for discussing state-of-the-art developments and future directions in smart technologies. Researchers and practitioners are invited to contribute high-quality theoretical and experimental work and join us in shaping the future of intelligent and sustainable technologies.

Conference Objectives

  • Foster cross-disciplinary collaboration between global researchers, engineers, and industry.
  • Highlight state-of-the-art solutions for clean, smart, and sustainable digital infrastructure.
  • Provide a rigorous peer-review evaluation environment for early-career and seasoned researchers.
  • Feature distinguished international keynote addresses and industry tutorials.

Academic Excellence

MITS is a premier self-financing technical institution offering top-tier postgraduate and undergraduate engineering programmes, committed to building a world-class centre of excellence focusing purely on merit.

Strategic Location

Situated in the industrial suburb of Kochi, close to the Smart City and InfoPark, approximately 15 kms from Vytilla Junction towards Muvattupuzha on the Cochin-Madurai National Highway.

Promoter Heritage

Backed by promoters with commanding presence in Financial Services, Hospitality, Healthcare, Renewable Energy, and Information Technology, upholding ethical values and high standard technical education.

State-of-the-Art Conference Facilities

MITS offers world-class air-conditioned auditoriums, advanced multimedia seminar halls, high-speed Wi-Fi, computer labs, and modern conference rooms designed to host international academic events with seamless technical presentation setups.
