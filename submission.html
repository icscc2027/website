Manuscript Specifications

All submissions must report original, previously unpublished research results, experimental data, theoretical developments, or novel technical solutions. Manuscripts must not be under simultaneous consideration for publication elsewhere.

  • Language: English is the official language of the conference. All submissions must be written in clear, concise technical English.
  • Page Limits: Regular papers should be 4 to 6 pages in standard double-column IEEE format. Extra pages (up to 2 additional pages) may be permitted subject to over-length page charges.
  • File Format: Initial manuscripts must be submitted exclusively in PDF format. Ensure all fonts are embedded.
  • Double-Blind Policy: The initial version of the paper submitted for peer review must NOT contain author names, affiliations, email addresses, or acknowledgements.

Plagiarism Policy & Publication Ethics

ICSCC 2027 enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy towards plagiarism and intellectual property infringement. All incoming manuscripts will be rigorously screened using CrossCheck / Turnitin anti-plagiarism tools.

Important: Papers with an overall similarity index exceeding 15% (or more than 3% from a single source, excluding bibliography) will be rejected immediately without peer review. Self-plagiarism is also strictly prohibited.
